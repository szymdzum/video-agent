# Taste — what Szymon likes in an edit

Living file. Read before planning any edit; append after every session (date + project).
Each entry: the rule, then *why / where it came from*. Prune rules he contradicts later.

## Story
- **There must be a guiding idea, not just shots on the beat.** Rhythmic cuts without a motif felt empty.
  *Tajland v1→v3 (2026-10-18): "ujęcia zmieniają się rytmicznie ale nie ma motywu".*
- **A journey arc works: land → port → channel → gate → open sea → exit.** Approach slowly, explore in the middle, leave at the end.
- **Don't drop into the middle too fast.** Establishing shot first, then a slow approach. Open water/action only after ~1 min.
- **The camera looks where we're going.** A shot facing back toward land while the story heads out to sea got cut.
- **Don't go backwards in the journey.** Don't cut from open water back to the channel.

## Shots
- **Only smooth, stable flights.** Skip the first seconds where the drone settles or shakes. He notices shake I can't measure (see LESSONS).
- **Shots with boats/wakes in them are the best material.** Stable boat follows beat empty landscape.
- **Fewer, longer shots.** 7–25 s per shot felt right; 3.5 s cuts all the way through felt like a slideshow. No need to use all the footage.
- **On a reveal, the subject enters the frame early.** Start the clip so the island appears during the move, not at the end of it.

## Music
- **Not stock.** Generic "cinematic / euphoric / travel" Suno prompts sounded stocky. Concrete local instruments did better (Thai: phin, khaen, ranat). He loved "psychedelic thai funk" as a genre tag.
- **The track needs dynamics:** quiet intro, breakdown, drop, long outro. A flat-energy track "didn't sit right" even when it sounded good.
- **Generates music in Suno himself.** I write the prompt (Style + structured `[Section: ...]` lyrics, `[Instrumental]`). He downloads WAV.

## Look
- **Grade A, "natural punch":** `eq=contrast=1.08:saturation=1.12,vibrance=intensity=0.25,cas=0.5`.
  Teal-water (B) oversaturated the sky; warm "cinematic" (C) came out cold/grey, rejected.

## Working style
- Small steps, gut-test every version, short feedback in Polish ("zacznij później", "wywal scenę 2").
- He gives timestamped notes per scene → map them to EDL shot indices and change only those.
